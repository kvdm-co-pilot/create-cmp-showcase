Fuelled · the design system, moving

Motion is a token, not a flourish.

Six durations, four curves, three springs and one arrival stagger — declared in Tokens.kt, read through a scheme that honours reduced motion by construction, and enforced by a conformance gate the same way a hardcoded colour is.

Every animation on this page was generated from those tokens. The springs are integrated from the same damped harmonic oscillator the Compose runtime integrates — same damping ratio, same stiffness, mass 1, and Compose’s own 0.01 visibility threshold for when an animation is over — then emitted as sampled CSS linear() easings. These are not a drawing of the app’s motion. They are its motion, replayed in a browser.

The ignition

The app’s first frame. A spark, the day ring, the mark, then seven letters 40 ms apart — and the ring hands off to Today’s hero ring as a shared element.

The duration scale

Six durations, six jobs, drawn on one time axis and filled at their real speeds. Nothing in the app animates for a length that is not one of these.

The curves

Three shaped curves and a linear one. Arriving uses Enter, leaving uses Exit; Standard carries state changes that neither arrive nor leave.

The springs

Not adjectives. Each panel plots the actual step response of a mass-1 oscillator at the token’s damping ratio and stiffness, and moves a puck along it at the speed the app would.

The arrival

One primitive behind every list in the app, with the cap that keeps a long day from crawling.

The bottom bar

Material 3’s NavigationBar, kept whole — ripple, Tab role, 80 dp, equal-weight cells — with one indicator that slides instead of five that cross-fade.

Reduced motion removes movement, not the moment

Three schemes, one composition local. Full is everything on this page. Reduced keeps every state change and every beat, and takes away the travel — the ignition becomes a quick fade of the assembled mark, held 900 ms so the moment still lands. Instant is what tests and previews run under: every animation is over on frame 0, so a golden tree is deterministic.

This page honours it too. With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, every figure above renders as its finished frame rather than its first one.
